Vannevar Bush's 1945 conceptual machine for personal knowledge storage and associative retrieval, described in his essay *As We May Think*.

## Core idea

A desk-sized device that would store an individual's books, records, and communications, linked by associative trails rather than hierarchical categories. Users could follow trails of related documents the way the human mind follows associations — a direct precursor to hypertext and modern personal knowledge management.

## Relevance to the LLM Wiki Pattern

The [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] is conceptually adjacent to Bush's vision: both are private, actively curated, and value the connections between documents as much as the documents themselves. Bush's unsolved problem was who would maintain those trails over time; the LLM pattern solves that by delegating maintenance to an LLM agent.
